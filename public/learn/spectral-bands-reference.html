HRV Spectral Bands

Quick reference · band definitions and interpretation caveats

Frequency bands per the 1996 Task Force standard.2 Ranges are fixed by convention, not by a physiological boundary — treat the edges as agreed cutoffs, not hard biological transitions.

Band Range Min. recording Often claimed to reflect Why to be careful
HF 0.15–0.4 Hz ~1–2 min Vagal (parasympathetic) tone, via respiratory sinus arrhythmia Amplitude is respiration-rate dependent, not a flat vagal gauge — see the resonance-curve argument in this repo's README.md.
LF 0.04–0.15 Hz ~2 min Sympathetic tone (historically) or baroreflex activity Mixed origin — ~50% parasympathetic, ~25% sympathetic contribution per Billman.1 Not a clean sympathetic marker.
LF/HF ratio — "Sympatho-vagal balance" Disproven as a balance index.1 Pharmacological blockade studies produce LF/HF shifts opposite to the true autonomic state.
VLF 0.0033–0.04 Hz ≥5 min (better: 24 h) Thermoregulation, renin-angiotensin system, intrinsic cardiac nervous system Mechanisms still genuinely uncertain per Shaffer & Ginsberg3 — don't over-claim, even with a valid recording length.
ULF ≤0.0033 Hz 24 h Circadian rhythm, core temperature, metabolism Meaningless outside a 24 h recording — a short-term simulator like this app's cannot show real ULF at all.
In this app src/sim/psd.ts integrates the actual PSD over each band rather than attributing "the breathing oscillator" to HF by assumption — see README.md, "Physiology model." This is why dragging breathing rate to 6/min visibly moves power from HF into LF while RMSSD rises: a live demonstration that LF is not a clean stand-in for sympathetic tone.
